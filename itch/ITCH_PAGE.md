# My Samurai Way: itch.io page

## Short description / tagline (itch "Short description or tagline")
A wandering ronin, a divided valley, three days and ten endings. A HD-2D samurai RPG where your words cut as deep as your sword.

---

## Description (English)

**You arrive in Kurogane-juku with a broken sword and an empty purse.**

The valley's black steel was once famous across the land. Now it's a town squeezed between two powers: the **Crow Gang** of Kuroda Jinbei, who rule the streets with blades, and **Magistrate Akamatsu**, who robs the village with stamps and tolls. The villagers just want to live.

You have **three days**. Who you help, who you betray and who you spare decides how the story of Kurogane ends.

### Features
- **HD-2D look.** Hand-pixelled sprites lit inside a 3D diorama, with depth of field, bloom, dynamic shadows, lantern light and falling sakura petals.
- **A living valley on a clock.** Every conversation costs time. Mornings are misty, afternoons golden, nights belong to lanterns, crickets and the Crow Gang. People move, shops close and new faces arrive.
- **Turn-based duels where you read your opponent.** Every turn your foe shows his intent: a quick cut, a heavy blow, a guard, a counter or a deadly technique. Parry the heavy blow, guard against the technique, break a guard with power. Beware: some fighters feint.
- **Bocas, the art of the insult.** Before you strike you can taunt your opponent. Every fighter has a hidden personality (Proud, Hot-headed, Cowardly, Stoic, Greedy) and the same words can break one man and fire up another:
  - Threaten a coward and he may surrender.
  - Threaten a proud man and he fights harder.
  - Insult him and he hits harder but drops his guard.
- **Choices that stick.** Spare or kill a beaten foe, join a faction, accept a contract, defend a stranger or side with her tormentors. The dead stay dead, and reputation with the three factions shapes who will talk to you.
- **10 endings,** from the valley's protector to the Crow's right hand, from a reforged black-steel blade to a village in flames. Your discovered endings are remembered between runs.
- **A journal** that tracks your reputation, deeds and discovered endings.
- **Original procedural soundtrack.** Koto, shakuhachi, taiko and shamisen in the Japanese miyako-bushi scale, plus a day and night soundscape. All synthesised in code.

This is an **early version**: one valley (Kurogane-juku), 3 days, 8 characters, 5 duelists and 10 endings. A run takes about 20–40 minutes. More valleys, characters and endings are planned. Feedback is very welcome!

*Made with Godot 4. All art, music and sound are generated procedurally in code.*

## Descrição (Português)

**Chegas a Kurogane-juku com uma espada partida e a bolsa vazia.**

O aço negro deste vale foi famoso em todo o país. Hoje Kurogane está esmagada entre dois poderes: o **Bando do Corvo** de Kuroda Jinbei, que manda nas ruas à espada, e o **Magistrado Akamatsu**, que rouba a vila com carimbos e portagens. A gente da vila só quer viver.

Tens **três dias**. Quem ajudas, quem trais e quem poupas decide como acaba a história de Kurogane.

### Características
- **Visual HD-2D.** Sprites em pixel-art iluminados num diorama 3D, com profundidade de campo, bloom, sombras dinâmicas, lanternas e pétalas de cerejeira.
- **Um vale vivo com relógio.** Cada conversa gasta tempo. As manhãs têm neblina, as tardes são douradas, e as noites pertencem às lanternas, aos grilos e ao Corvo.
- **Duelos por turnos em que lês o adversário.** Ele mostra a intenção (golpe rápido, golpe forte, guarda, contra-ataque, técnica) e tu respondes. Alguns fintam.
- **Bocas.** Antes de atacar, mandas uma boca. Cada adversário tem uma personalidade escondida (Orgulhoso, Colérico, Cobarde, Estoico, Ganancioso), e a mesma frase pode partir um homem e enfurecer outro.
- **Escolhas com peso.** Poupar ou matar, juntar-te a uma facção, aceitar contratos, defender ou humilhar. Os mortos não voltam.
- **10 finais**, guardados entre partidas, e um **diário** com reputação e acontecimentos.
- **Banda sonora procedural original**, com koto, shakuhachi, taiko e shamisen.

### Controlos
WASD/setas para andar · Shift para correr · E/Espaço para falar e confirmar · Esc para voltar · Tab para o diário.

*Versão inicial: um vale, 3 dias e 10 finais. Feito em Godot 4, com arte, música e som gerados por código.*
